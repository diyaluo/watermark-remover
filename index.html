Frequently Asked Questions

How does the watermark removal work? ▼
Upload your image, use the brush tool to paint over the watermark areas (they\'ll turn red), then click "Remove Watermark". The tool uses OpenCV\'s inpainting algorithm to intelligently fill the selected areas based on surrounding pixels.
Is my image uploaded to a server? ▼
No! All processing happens entirely in your browser using OpenCV.js. Your images never leave your device. This is our core privacy advantage over other watermark removal tools.
What image formats are supported? ▼
JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF are supported. The output is downloaded as PNG to preserve quality.
Are there any usage limits? ▼
No limits whatsoever. You can remove watermarks from as many images as you want, completely free. No registration or sign-up required.
